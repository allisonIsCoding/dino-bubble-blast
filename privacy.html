🦖 Dino Bubble Blast Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Dino Bubble Blast is a game made by Allison. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it on iPhone, iPad or the web.

The short version

The game does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

To save your progress, the game keeps a few small pieces of information on your own device: your current level, score and hearts, your best score, the ship you picked, and whether you have unlocked the full game. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app (or clearing your browser's site data) removes it.

Purchases

The full game unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and your payment details, and we never see them. Apple only tells the game whether your Apple Account owns the unlock, so it can be restored on your other devices. Apple's own privacy policy applies to purchases: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Web version

The free web version is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its font from Google Fonts. Like any website, GitHub and Google may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when the page loads. The game itself does not collect anything. The iPhone and iPad app includes its font and does not contact Google.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
